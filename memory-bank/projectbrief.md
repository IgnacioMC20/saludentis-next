# Project Brief: Saludentis

## Overview
Saludentis is a comprehensive dental clinic management system built with Next.js. The application provides a complete solution for dental professionals to manage patients, treatments, diagnoses, and clinic operations.

## Core Requirements

### Patient Management
- Patient registration and profile management
- Medical history tracking
- Appointment scheduling
- Treatment planning and tracking
- Financial management (balances, quotations)

### Clinical Tools
- Digital odontogram (dental chart) for visual tooth mapping
- Diagnostic tools
- Treatment planning
- Diet and nutrition tracking
- Clinical history documentation

### Administrative Features
- User authentication and authorization
- Disease and treatment catalog management
- Financial tracking and reporting

## Technical Goals
- Create a responsive, modern web application
- Ensure data security and patient privacy
- Provide an intuitive user interface for dental professionals
- Enable efficient workflow for dental clinic operations
- Support comprehensive patient record management

## Target Users
- Dentists and dental specialists
- Dental clinic administrative staff
- Dental assistants

## Success Criteria
- Streamlined patient management process
- Reduced administrative overhead
- Improved treatment tracking and planning
- Enhanced patient experience through better record keeping
- Efficient financial management for dental services
